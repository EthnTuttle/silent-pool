## What changed

- `spc-lab-self-hosted-ark` now passes `--pow-bits 0x2000cccc` (1 in 320
  hashes; about 40 s per block at the scenario's 8 H/s) instead of running the
  CLI default `0x200fffff` (1 in 16; one block every 3.5 s observed). A
  caller's own `--pow-bits` replaces the wrapper default: the wrapper drops
  its default when the argument list already carries the flag, because
  `spc-lab runtime` rejects a repeated `--pow-bits`.
- `lab-ark-wallets.json` raises `share_work_multiplier` from 2 to 16 (shares
  at 1 in 32 hashes, about ten per block). `reference_target`, `window_rule`,
  `window_n` and `max_slice_shares` are unchanged.
- The `spc-lab runtime` CLI default, every other scenario, and the AP5
  prepared campaign (`testing/lab-ark-funded`, which launches the lab
  executable directly and requires `initial_target_bits = 200fffff`) are
  unchanged.

## Why

Regtest halves the 50 BTC subsidy every 150 blocks and the lab node has no
flag for that. At 3.5 s per block the subsidy fell below the 5M-sat top
ladder rung after about 1.5 h and below dust after about 3.5 h. At about 40 s
per block the 1500 blocks above the top rung take about 16.7 h.

The share multiplier follows from the cadence. Evidence records embed the full
coinbase (about 990 ladder outputs while the subsidy is 50 BTC), and the slower
cadence stretches that era from 9 minutes to 100. With shares left at 1 in 4
hashes the run directory grew 8.6 GB per hour.
